Fixes in flight: [PR #3140](https://github.com/kiro-team/kiro-cli/pull/3140), autocomplete [PR #650](https://github.com/kiro-team/kiro-cli-autocomplete/pull/650).
* [P449035647](https://t.corp.amazon.com/P449035647) — **Systemic `AgentLoopError(EmptyResponse)` on subagent dispatch.** PR #2737's "fail on second consecutive empty response" turned Bedrock `content_filtered` into a hard failure, blocking the PartnerSupportAutomation pipeline and multiple internal teams. Backend confirmed `content_filtered` rate unchanged; fix belongs on CLI side (raise threshold / handle stop reason). Workaround: pin to 2.5.0.
* [P457508516](https://t.corp.amazon.com/P457508516) — **kiro-cli v2.8.1 sessions spontaneously suspend.** A `cr` invocation through `zsh -ic` reclaims terminal foreground, sending SIGTTIN to the kiro process group every ~5 min. Reproduced; fix is to drop the `-i` interactive flag in the ACP shell-command exec path.
